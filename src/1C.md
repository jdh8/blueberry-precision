# Strong 1♣

Blueberry responds to 1♣ with the transfers of [Viking Precision 2007][vp]:
each positive from 1♥ to 2♦ shows the other suit of its rank.  See
[transfer positives](#transfer-positives) for how the strong hand comes to
declare and how the relays fit together.

## Transfer positives

The longest suit decides the response.  Equal majors respond 1♥, and a major
beats a minor of equal length.  A (5332) hand responds in its 5-card major,
otherwise 1NT, so a minor positive never holds (5332).  A 4-card minor beside a
5+ minor responds 2♥, so a minor positive never holds a second minor either.

Opener relays with the cheapest bid.  The relays 1♠ and 2♦ bid responder's
suit, and the 1♣ opening already bid clubs, so the strong hand declares
those three suits however the relay ends.  Hearts are the exception, so
1♣ - 1♠ - 2♥ completes the transfer with a fit instead of relaying.  Viking
has no such bid, and 1♣ - 1♠ - 1NT - 2♥ lets the described hand declare.

Responder's strength is 8--12 or 13+.  Over a major, 13+ bids 2♣ after the
relay and then gives the same shape answers as 8--12 does directly.  Over a
minor, 13+ bids its shape at once, and 8--12 bids the cheapest step and gives
the same shape one step higher after the next relay.

Each lattice in these chapters maps every shape still possible at that point
of the relay to its bid, one lattice per length of the suit in its corner
label.  Of the other three suits, the highest ranking grows upwards, the next
down-left and the lowest down-right.  A lattice on a single line is laid flat,
with arrows naming the suit that grows each way.  Borders mark where the bid
changes.  [`relay.py`][relay] draws them and checks that no shape is missed or
claimed twice.

[relay]: https://github.com/jdh8/blueberry-precision/blob/main/relay.py

## Three-suited 2♠, 2NT

declares.
