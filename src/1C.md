# Strong 1♣

Blueberry follows [IMprecision][im] by Adam Meyerson and Samuel Ieong after
1♣.  The structure mixes relays and natural bidding to cater better for
intermediate hands opposite 1♣.  Relay points (RP) count A = 3, K = 2, and
Q = 1.  Responder's hand falls into one of four categories:

| Responder      | |
|----------------|-|
| Super-positive | 7+ RP
| Weak positive  | 2--6 RP, game-forcing values
| Semi-positive  | 2--6 RP, less than game values
| Negative       | None of the above

[im]: http://www.infobridge.it/Sistemi_IMprecision.pdf

All negative and super-positive hands respond 1♦.  So do balanced positive
hands and hands that prefer asking to telling.  The other responses show
shape.  A 5332 hand is balanced only with a 5-card minor.

| 1♣ - | |
|------|-|
| 1♦!  | NEG or super-positive or BAL positive or hands preferring to ask
| 1♥!  | 4+♠, (UNBAL or BAL semi-positive)
| 1♠!  | 5+♦ or (BAL semi-positive, 0--3♠)
| 1NT! | 5+♥, 0--3♠, 0--4♣
| 2♣   | UNBAL 5+♣, 0--3♠, 0--3♥
| 2♦!  | 5+♣, 4+♥
| 2♥!  | Three-suited, 1444 or 0(445)
| 2♠!  | Three-suited, 4441 or (445)0
| 2NT! | FG, 3--6 RP, (322)6
| 3♣!  | FG, 3--6 RP, 2227 or 2272
| 3♦!  | FG, 3--6 RP, 2263
| 3♥!  | FG, 3--6 RP, 2362
| 3♠!  | FG, 3--4 RP, 3262
| 3NT! | FG, 5--6 RP, 3262
| 4♣!  | PRE, S-SOL 7+♠♥, 0--1 outside A/K
| 4♦!  | PRE, SOL 7+♠♥, no outside A/K
| 4♥♠  | PRE, 7+#

After 1♥ through 2♠, opener's cheapest suit bid is a relay.  It forces to game
except [1♣ - 1♥ - 1♠](1C/1H.md).  Responder then resolves shape along the
[relay structure](1C/Relay.md).  Most other rebids by opener are natural.
