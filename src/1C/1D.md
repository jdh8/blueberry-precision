# Two-way 1♦

Opener usually rebids at the 1-level.  Responder relays with a game-forcing
hand.  Otherwise, responder scrambles, or passes a non-forcing rebid.

| 1♣ - 1♦ - | |
|------|-|
| 1♥!  | F1, UNBAL 4+♥ or (BAL 21+, 0--3♠)
| 1♠   | F1, 4+♠, UNBAL or BAL 21+
| 1NT  | 17--20, BAL or nearly so
| 2♣   | 16--20, 0--3♠, 6+♣ or (5+♣, 4=♦♥)
| 2♦   | 16--20, 0--3♠, 0--3♥, 6+♦ or (5=♦, 4=♣)
| 2♥!  | F1, 21+, 4=♥, 5+♣
| 2♠!  | 16+, 5+♦, 5+♣
| 2NT! | 21+, 6+♦♣, 0--3♠, 0--3♥, not FG if UNBAL
| 3♣♦  | FG, short in the other minor
| 3♥♠  | FG, short in the other major, one or both minors

An unbalanced 1♥ may hold longer diamonds but rarely a longer black suit.  An
unbalanced 1♠ may hold a longer minor but not longer hearts except 4504.
Three-suited hands rebid the major of the opposite color to the shortness.

Systems are on over 1NT.

The [relay](Relay.md) puts opener on tracks similar to
[1♣ - 1♥ - 1♠](1H.md), except that one-suiters swap with major two-suiters
and resolve directly.  All other hands lose a step.
