# Negative 1♦

Relay structure from [Viking Precision 2007][vp] nest very strong hands into
1♣ - 1♦ - 1♥.  This treatment rescues the 2NT opening from the notrump ladder and
frees 2NT up for preemptive uses.

[vp]: https://bridge.downagain.be/FD/main.php?vul=9&dealer=9&system=smp7cltvhrh

### Positive major 1NT

When several rows fit a hand, the row for its longest suit applies.

Each lattice below maps every shape still possible at that point of the relay
to its bid, one lattice per ♠ length, ♥ up, ♦ down-left, ♣ down-right.  Borders
mark where the bid changes.  [`relay.py`][relay] draws them and checks that no
shape is missed or claimed twice.

[relay]: https://github.com/jdh8/blueberry-precision/blob/main/relay.py
