# Spades 1♥

1♣ - 1♥ shows 5+♠, or BAL 13+ without a 5-card major.  Opener relays with
1♠.

| 1♣ - 1♥ - | |
|-----|-|
| 1♠! | (R)

| 1♣ - 1♥ -<br>1♠ - | |
|------|-|
| 1NT! | BAL 13+, no 5-card major
| 2♣!  | 13+, 5+♠
| 2♦!  | 8--12, 5+♠, 4=♥ or (5332)
| 2♥   | 8--12, 5=♠, 5=♥
| 2♠   | 8--12, 6+♠, no side suit
| 2NT! | 8--12, 6+♠, 4=♦♣
| 3♣♦  | 8--12, 5=♠, 4--5#
| 3♥!  | 8--12, 6+♠, 5=♣
| 3♠!  | 8--12, 6+♠, 5=♦
| 3NT! | 8--12, 6+♠, 5+♥
| 4♣♦  | 8--12, 6+♠, 6=#

> [!NOTE]
>
> Viking's 2♦ shows 5♠ with a 4--6 card minor, and its 2♥ is balanced or 4♥.
> Its card lists both 3♣ and 3♦ as 5♠ + 5♣ and has no bid for 5♠ + 5♥.

With 6+♠ and a 4-card minor, opener relays to ask which.

| 1♣ - 1♥ -<br>1♠ - 2NT - | |
|----------|-|
| 3♣!      | (R)
| 3♣ - 3♦! | 4=♣
| 3♣ - 3♥! | 4=♦

The balanced 1NT relays as [1♣ - 1NT](1NT.md) does.  After 2♣, opener relays
with 2♦ and responder gives the 8--12 answers, except that 2♥ shows 4♥ or
(5332) and 3NT shows 5-5 as well.

| 1♣ - 1♥ -<br>1♠ - 2♣ - | |
|-----------|-|
| 2♦!       | (R)
| 2♦ - 2♥!  | 4=♥ or (5332)
| 2♦ - 3NT! | 5+♠, 5+♥

> [!NOTE]
>
> Viking's 3♣ and 3♦ here show exactly 4 in the minor, and its 3NT is 6-5 in the
> majors.
