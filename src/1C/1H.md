# Spades 1♥

1♣ - 1♥ shows 4+♠, unbalanced or a balanced semi-positive.  The 1♠ relay is
not necessarily game-forcing.  It shows either game-forcing values or a 5-card
or longer major, and 6+♥ implies spade tolerance as well.  Other rebids are
natural and tend to deny such hands.

| 1♣ - 1♥ - | |
|------|-|
| 1♠!  | (R), FG or 5+♠♥
| 1NT  | NAT, 17--19
| 2♣♦  | NAT
| 2♥   | One-suited 6+♥, usually no interest in ♠
| 2♠!  | Both minors, any strength
| 2NT  | Not quite FG, long minor, wants to declare notrump
| Else | NAT, one-suited, close to game values

Systems are on over 1NT.  Over 2♣, continue like the [2♣ opening](../2C.md)
with 2♥ and 2♠ swapped.  IMprecision does the same over 2♦, but Blueberry has
no natural 2♦ opening.  Bid naturally over 2♦.
