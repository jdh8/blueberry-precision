# Spades 1♥

1♣ - 1♥ shows 5+♠, or BAL 13+ without a 5-card major.  Opener relays with
1♠.

| 1♣ - 1♥ - | |
|-----|-|
| 1♠! | (R)

| 1♣ - 1♥ -<br>1♠ - | |
|------|-|
| 1NT! | BAL 13+, no 5-card major
| 2♣!  | 13+, 5+♠, no (5=♠, 5+♦♣)
| 2♦!  | 8--12, 5=♠, 4+♦♣
| 2♥!  | 8--12, 5+♠, 4=♥ or (5332)
| 2♠   | 8--12, 6+♠, no side suit
| 2NT! | 8--12, (6+♠, 4=♦♣) or (5=♠, 5=♥)
| 3♣!  | 13+, 5=♠, 5+♦
| 3♦!  | 13+, 5=♠, 5+♣
| 3♥!  | 8--12, 6+♠, 5=♣
| 3♠!  | 8--12, 6+♠, 5=♦
| 3NT! | 8--12, 6+♠, 5=♥
| 4♣♦♥ | 8--12, 6+♠, 6+#

Viking's card lists 3♣ and 3♦ as 5♠ + 5♣ and has no bid for 5♠ + 5♥.  It
also lists 3♠ as 12+, but its 2♣ - 2♦ - 3♠ shows the same 6♠ + 5♦.

The balanced 1NT relays as [1♣ - 1NT](1NT.md) does.

## Strong 2♣

Opener relays with 2♦ and responder gives the 8--12 answers, except for 5♠
with a minor and 3NT also taking 5♠ + 5♥.

| 1♣ - 1♥ -<br>1♠ - 2♣ - | |
|-----------------------------------|-|
| 2♦!                               | (R)
| [2♦ - 2♥!](#hearts-or-balanced-2) | 5+♠, 4=♥ or (5332)
| [2♦ - 2♠](#one-suited-2)          | 6+♠, no side suit
| [2♦ - 2NT!](#two-suited-2nt)      | 6+♠, 4=♦♣
| 2♦ - 3♣♦                          | 5=♠, 4=#
| 2♦ - 3♥!                          | 6+♠, 5=♣
| 2♦ - 3♠!                          | 6+♠, 5=♦
| 2♦ - 3NT!                         | 5+♠, 5=♥
| 2♦ - 4♣♦♥                         | 6+♠, 6+#

Viking's 3NT here is 6-5 in the majors.
