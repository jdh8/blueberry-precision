# Spades 1♥

1♣ - 1♥ shows 5+♠, or BAL 13+ without a 5-card major.  Opener relays with
1♠.

| 1♣ - 1♥ - | |
|-----|-|
| 1♠! | (R)

| 1♣ - 1♥ -<br>1♠ - | |
|------|-|
| 1NT! | BAL 13+, no 5-card major
| 2♣!  | 13+, 5+♠
| 2♦!  | 8--12, (5332)
| 2♥   | 8--12, 5+♠, 4+♥
| 2♠   | 8--12, 6+♠, no side suit
| 2NT! | 8--12, 6+♠, 4=♦♣
| 3♣♦  | 8--12, 5=♠, 4--5#
| 3♥!  | 8--12, 6+♠, 5=♣
| 3♠!  | 8--12, 6+♠, 5=♦
| 3NT! | 8--12, 5+♠, 5+♥
| 4♣♦  | 8--12, 6+♠, 6=#

The balanced 1NT relays as [1♣ - 1NT](1NT.md) does.  After 2♣, opener relays
with 2♦ and responder gives the 8--12 answers, except that 2♥ also holds
(5332).

| 1♣ - 1♥ -<br>1♠ - 2♣ - | |
|----------|-|
| 2♦!      | (R)
| 2♦ - 2♥! | 4+♥ or (5332)
