# Hearts 1♠

| 1♣ - 1♠ - | |
|------|-|
| 1NT! | (R)

| 1♣ - 1♠ -<br>1NT - | |
|------|-|
| 2♣!  | 13+, 5+♥, no (5=♥, 5+♦♣)
| 2♦!  | 8--12, 5=♥, 4+♦♣
| 2♥!  | 8--12, 5+♥, 4=♠ or (5332)
| 2♠!  | 8--12, 6+♥, no side suit
| 2NT! | 8--12, 6+♥, 4=♦♣
| 3♣!  | 13+, 5=♥, 5+♦

| 3♠!  | 8--12, 6+♥, 5=♦

| 4♣♦  | 8--12, 6+♥, 6=#

Viking has 3♦ at 8--12.  I adjusted it here to 13+ because 2♦ already handles
8--12 well.
