# Hearts 1♠

1♣ - 1♠ shows 5+♥ and a shorter spade suit.  Opener relays with 1NT or
completes the transfer with 2♥.

| 1♣ - 1♠ - | |
|------|-|
| 1NT! | (R)
| 2♥!  | 3+♥

> [!NOTE]
>
> Viking has no 2♥, so 1♣ - 1♠ - 1NT - 2♥ lets the described hand declare.

| 1♣ - 1♠ -<br>1NT - | |
|------|-|
| 2♣!  | 13+, 5+♥
| 2♦!  | 8--12, 5+♥, 4=♠ or (5332)
| 2♠!  | 8--12, 6+♥, no side suit
| 2NT! | 8--12, 6+♥, 4=♦♣
| 3♣♦  | 8--12, 5=♥, 4--5#
| 3♥!  | 8--12, 6+♥, 5=♣
| 3♠!  | 8--12, 6+♥, 5=♦
| 3NT! | 8--12, 6+♥, 5=♠
| 4♣♦  | 8--12, 6+♥, 6=#

> [!NOTE]
>
> Viking's 2♦ shows 5♥ with a 4--6 card minor, and its 2♥ is balanced or 4♠.
> Its card lists 3♣ as 5♥ + 5♦ and 3♦ as 5♥ + 5♣.

With 6+♥ and a 4-card minor, opener relays to ask which.

| 1♣ - 1♠ -<br>1NT - 2NT - | |
|----------|-|
| 3♣!      | (R)
| 3♣ - 3♦! | 4=♣
| 3♣ - 3♥! | 4=♦

After 2♣, opener relays with 2♦ and responder gives the 8--12 answers, except
that 2♥ shows 4♠ or (5332).

| 1♣ - 1♠ -<br>1NT - 2♣ - | |
|----------|-|
| 2♦!      | (R)
| 2♦ - 2♥! | 4=♠ or (5332)

> [!NOTE]
>
> Viking's 3♣ and 3♦ here show exactly 4 in the minor.  Its card lists 3♥ as
> 5♥ + 5♣ and has no bid for 6♥ + 5♦.
