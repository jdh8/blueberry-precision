# Diamonds or balanced 1♠

1♣ - 1♠ shows 5+♦, or a balanced semi-positive without 4♠.  Opener's 2♣ is
the game-forcing relay, and 1NT is natural.

| 1♣ - 1♠ - | |
|-----|-|
| 1NT | NAT, 17--19
| 2♣! | (R) FG
| 2♦! | 16--19, 6+♠♥ or (4=♥, longer suit) or 1444
| 2♥♠ | 16--19, 5+#, 4+ side suit
| 2NT | 16--19, 5+ 4+ minors
| 3♣♦ | 16--19, 6+#

Systems are on over 1NT.

## The 2♣ relay

| 1♣ - 1♠ -<br>2♣ - | |
|------|-|
| 2♦!  | BAL
| 2♥   | 4=♥, 5+♦
| 2♠!  | 4=♣, 5+♦, two-suited table losing a step
| 2NT+ | One-suited 6+♦, losing a step but skipping 5332

| 1♣ - 1♠ -<br>2♣ - 2♦ - | |
|----------|-|
| 2♥!      | (R)
| 2♥ - 2♠  | 2=♠, symmetric with the steps below
| 2♥ - 2NT | 3433 or 3343 or 3334, resolved numerically
| 2♥ - 3♣  | 3=♠, 5=♣; relay asks for shortness
| 2♥ - 3♦  | 3=♠, 5=♦; relay asks for shortness
| 2♥ - 3♥  | 3244
| 2♥ - 3♠  | 3424
| 2♥ - 3NT | 3442
| 2NT      | NAT FG, [Keri-style continuations](Relay.md#keri-style-breaks)
| 3♣♦♥♠!   | Three-suited, short in the other suit of the same rank
