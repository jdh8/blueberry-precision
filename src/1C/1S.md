# Hearts 1♠

1♣ - 1♠ shows 5+♥ and a shorter spade suit.  Opener relays with 1NT or
completes the transfer with 2♥.

| 1♣ - 1♠ - | |
|------|-|
| 1NT! | (R)
| 2♥!  | 3+♥

| 1♣ - 1♠ -<br>1NT - | |
|------|-|
| 2♣!  | 13+, 5+♥
| 2♦!  | 8--12, (5332)
| 2♥!  | 8--12, 5+♥, 4=♠
| 2♠!  | 8--12, 6+♥, no side suit
| 2NT! | 8--12, 6+♥, 4=♦♣
| 3♣♦  | 8--12, 5=♥, 4--5#
| 3♥!  | 8--12, 6+♥, 5=♣
| 3♠!  | 8--12, 6+♥, 5=♦
| 3NT! | 8--12, 6+♥, 5=♠
| 4♣♦  | 8--12, 6+♥, 6=#

After 2♣, opener relays with 2♦ and responder gives the 8--12 answers, except
that 2♥ also holds (5332).

| 1♣ - 1♠ -<br>1NT - 2♣ - | |
|----------|-|
| 2♦!      | (R)
| 2♦ - 2♥! | 4=♠ or (5332)
