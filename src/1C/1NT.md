# Hearts 1NT

1♣ - 1NT shows 5+♥ without 4♠ or 5+♣.  Opener's 2♣ is the game-forcing
relay.

| 1♣ - 1NT - | |
|-----|-|
| 2♣! | (R) FG
| 2♦! | Not FG, 5+♠
| 2♥  | MIN, usually 2=♥
| 2♠! | 5+♦, 5+♣
| 2NT | NF, three-suited, short in ♥
| 3♣♦ | NF, 6+#, 0--1♥
| 3♥  | Very MIN, 3=♥

| 1♣ - 1NT -<br>2♣ - | |
|-----|-|
| 2♦! | 4+♦, not a reverser; then two-suited table
| 2♥  | 5+♥, 4=♣; then two-suited table
| 2♠+ | One-suited 5+♥, on track

| 1♣ - 1NT -<br>2♦ - | |
|------|-|
| 2♥   | NF, MIN, 0--1♠
| 2♠   | NF, MIN, 2=♠
| 2NT  | FG, 2=♠
| 3♣   | FG, 0--1♠, 4=♣
| 3♦   | FG, 0--1♠, 4+♦
| 3♥   | FG, 0--1♠, 6+♥
| 3♠   | NF, MIN, 3=♠
| 3NT  | FG COG, 3=♠, 6=♥
| 4♣♦! | FG SPL, 3=♠

| 1♣ - 1NT -<br>2♥ - | |
|------|-|
| P    | Not FG, no extra ♥
| 2♠!  | INV, 5=♥, 5=♦, choice of contract
| 3♥   | Re-invite, 6+♥
| Else | NAT FG

Responder relays over 2♠ and opener describes.

| 1♣ - 1NT -<br>2♠ - | |
|------|-|
| 2NT! | (R) FG
| 3♣♦♥ | NF, suggests a contract
| 3♠!  | Asks for ♠ help for 3NT
