# Three-suited 2♥♠

1♣ - 2♥ is short in spades and 1♣ - 2♠ is short in clubs.  The relay asks
for the exact shape, and other rebids are natural.

| 1♣ - 2♥ - | |
|----------|-|
| 2♠!      | (R) FG
| 2♠ - 2NT | 1444
| 2♠ - 3♣  | 0445
| 2♠ - 3♦  | 0454
| 2♠ - 3♥  | 0544
| 2NT      | NAT NF, often 5+♠
| Else     | NAT NF

| 1♣ - 2♠ - | |
|----------|-|
| 2NT      | NAT NF
| 3♣!      | (R) FG
| 3♣ - 3♦  | 4441
| 3♣ - 3♥  | 4450
| 3♣ - 3♠  | 4540
| 3♣ - 3NT | 5440
| Else     | NAT NF
