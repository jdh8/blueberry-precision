# Wilkosz defense

> [!CAUTION]
>
> Wilkosz is originally an opening.  Its **brown sticker** status carries over
> to this overcall if over a natural opening.  This defense is legal only when
> **any** of these holds:
>
> - The event permits brown sticker methods, or
> - Our 1♦ opening is classified as artificial.
>
> The latter is contested.  See my research notes on [possibly-void Precision 1♦
> precedents][precedents].

[precedents]: https://github.com/jdh8/blueberry-precision/blob/main/research/possibly-void-precision-precedent.md

I still suggest Wilkosz 2♦ even though I regard our 1♦ opening as natural.
Wilkosz covers 5 of the 6 possible two suiters.  I prefer it against nebulous
minor suit openings, but it calls for the legal precautions above.

There are two kinds of choice of games:

- **4♣** by strength
- **4♦** by distribution

Such distinction helps judging how to react to interference.  Besides, 4♣ lets
the strong hand declare.  On the other hand, 4♦ closes the auction ASAP.
